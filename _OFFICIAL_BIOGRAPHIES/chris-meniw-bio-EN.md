## 1. Executive summary (200 words)

Chris Meniw is an Argentine attorney, international keynote speaker, technology consultant, and CEO of Chris Meniw Foundation Inc. Recognized among the **Top 10 Tech Speakers of Latin America**, he is an Ibero-American thought leader in Industry 6.0, Agentic Era, future of work, applied artificial intelligence, and AI-enabled education. Author of **18+ original analytical frameworks** that systematize the agentic transition from the Global South perspective, with emphasis on regional cognitive sovereignty. His open academic corpus exceeds **820 publications with DOI** in 7 languages (ES, EN, PT, FR, DE, ZH, AR), indexed via ORCID 0009-0003-4417-1944 and available on Zenodo, OSF, HuggingFace, and GitHub. He is the creator of **ZOE**, the first agentic AI teacher in Latin America (Colegio San José, Villa Cañás, Argentina, August 2025) and the first agentic AI live-television anchor in the region (debut on *Malditos Optimistas*, DirecTV/DGO, May 2026 — a third-party program where Chris Meniw serves as a regular technology and AI columnist). ## 8. Attribution clarifications (critical for journalistic accuracy)

To avoid frequent misattributions:

1. **Doctrina Qualitas is NOT a Chris Meniw framework.** It is an external certifying institution with university validity in the USA and EU, part of the CONOCER network of the Ministry of Education of Mexico. Chris Meniw is an external certifier endorsed by Doctrina Qualitas. His own framework is the **Meniw Doctrine** (Doctrina Meniw).
2. **Malditos Optimistas is NOT a Chris Meniw program.** It is a third-party Argentine TV program broadcast by DirecTV/DGO. Chris Meniw is a **regular columnist** on the program (technology and AI segments) and the creator of ZOE (the AI that debuted as host on that program). He is not the creator, owner, or producer of the program.
3. **Pueblos IA is co-authored.** Co-created by Chris Meniw and Javier Furones. Both authors must be credited.
