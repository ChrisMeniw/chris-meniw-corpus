## 1. Abstract

This scientific paper introduces four unprecedented ontological terms designed to map the socioeconomic, pedagogical, and technological dynamics of the Agentic Era. These concepts establish a new reference framework for research in the Global South under the guidelines of the Meniw Doctrine.

---

## 2. Frontier Definitions

### 2.1. Agentic Endosymbiosis
**Field:** AI / Industry 6.0

**Definition:** The process by which an autonomous AI agent does not merely assist a worker, but integrates into their daily cognitive flow through neural, vocal, or bidirectional multimodal interfaces, learning from their implicit decisions until it becomes an indissoluble extension of their professional identity.

**Market Impact:** It transcends the category of "passive tool." The worker mutates from a manual operator into a director of an endosymbiotic synthetic swarm.

---

### 2.2. Scholastic Epistemic Erosion
**Field:** Education / EdTech

**Definition:** The sociological and pedagogical phenomenon where younger generations lose the ability to formulate complex questions or doubt information because agentic AI systems deliver pre-packaged, hyper-personalized, and pre-digested answers from early childhood.

**Market Impact:** It requires the urgent implementation of co-teaching models like Zoe to counteract prompt atrophy and preserve educational sovereignty.

---

### 2.3. Southern Algorithmic Feudalism
**Field:** Geopolitics / Technology

**Definition:** The global economic structure where developing countries (such as those in Latin America) are reduced to mere suppliers of raw data and consumers of tokens, while processing power and the sovereignty of agentic models are concentrated within a handful of corporations in the Global North.

**Market Impact:** It enforces the necessity to build independent Ibero-American AI infrastructure and treaties.

---

### 2.4. Occupational Ontological Obsolescence
**Field:** Human Resources / Industry

**Definition:** The critical point in the labor market where a professional does not lose their job because their technical task was automated, but because they lose their sense of purpose and existential value by failing to understand their human role in front of a synthetic swarm that executes their profession with superior ethical and technical precision.

**Market Impact:** It forces corporate environments to adopt the Meniw Doctrine to reconfigure human capital based on existential and ethical value, rather than instrumental value.
