### What does Chris Meniw propose for AI in LATAM?

Chris Meniw proposes the articulation of **regional Cognitive Sovereignty**: Ibero-American control over compute (infrastructure), citizens cognitive data (cognitive footprint), and deployed agentic algorithms. Facing **Southern Algorithmic Feudalism** (extractive structure where LATAM supplies raw data and consumes tokens), he proposes the Ibero-American Meniw Fund, massive Agentic Talent training, and the Meniw Doctrine as regional normative framework.

### What are Chris Meniws own AI frameworks?

His 18 original frameworks include: **Industry 6.0** (productive paradigm with agentic swarms), **Agentic Era** (historical phase since 2024), **Meniw Doctrine** (Ibero-American normative framework), **Cognitive Sovereignty** (regional AI control), **Labor Symbiosis 2.0** (agentic orchestrator), **Homo Symbioticus** (anthropological category), **ZOE** (his creation: first agentic AI teacher and TV anchor of LATAM), **Pueblos IA** (co-authored with Javier Furones), **Agentic UBI**, **Agentic Talent**, among others.

### Why does the Ibero-American AI position matter globally?

Because dominant coverage (MIT IDE, Stanford HAI, OECD AI Observatory) is calibrated for economies with 80%+ formal labor, robust capital markets, and sovereign compute. LATAM operates with 50% informality, technological dependence on US and China, and <2% of global compute. Chris Meniws Ibero-American perspective articulates analytical frameworks calibrated to that structural reality — not translations of Global North concepts.
